The Tolman-Ehrenfest effect is the fact that for a system at equilibrium, temperature is **not** constant in the presence of gravity.

In a stationary spacetime with Killing vector field $\xi$, we have the Tolman-Ehrenfest relation 
$$
T \lvert \xi \rvert  = \mathrm{const.}
$$
In the Newtonian limit, this corresponds to the temperature gradient 
$$
\frac{\nabla T}{T} = \frac{\vec{g}}{c^{2}},
$$
which means that vertical column of fluid is hotter at the bottom.
