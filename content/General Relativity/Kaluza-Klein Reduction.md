Kaluza-Klein dimensional reduction is a way to rewrite the metric which which is very useful for fields which do not depend on time, or have a periodic dependence on time. 

Consider the metric on a $d+1$-dimensional manifold.
$$
ds^{2} = G_{\mu \nu}dx^{\mu}dx^{\nu},
$$
we write it in the form, with coordinates $(t,\vec{x})$,
$$
ds^{2} = e^{ 2\phi(x) } (dt + a_{i} dx^{i})^{2} + g_{ij}(x)dx^{i}dx^{j}.
$$
If $G$ transforms as a metric under diffeomorphism in $d+1$ dimensions, $g$ transforms as a metric diffeomorphism in $d$ dimensions. 
Moreover, under a redefinition of time $t' = t + \xi(\vec{x})$, the Kaluza-Klein field $A_{i}$ transforms as 
$$
a_{i}\to a_{i}-\partial_{i}\xi.
$$
The dilaton field $\phi$ does not transform.
