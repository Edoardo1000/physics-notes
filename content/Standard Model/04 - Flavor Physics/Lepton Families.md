We assume that we have *massless neutrinos*.
By looking at the Yukawa interaction for leptons with three flavors, we see that the matrix $Y_{e}$ can be **diagonalized**.

 In this case, the matrix $Y_{e}$ can be **diagonalized**, and we still have the *residual symmetry* 
$$
 U(1)_{e} \times U(1){\mu} \times U(1)_{\tau}.
$$

From this symmetry group, we see that **single lepton flavors are conserved**. Note that this is not a fundamental law, single lepton number conservation is *accidental*.
