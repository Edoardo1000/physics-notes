An alternative model for [[Electroweak Mass Generation|vector boson mass generation]] was proposed by Georgi and Glashow. Thus, it often called the *Georgi-Glashow model*.

In this model, the Higgs is a triplet 
$$
	\Phi = \Phi^{a}T^{a}, \quad\langle \Phi \rangle = (0,v,0),
$$
which is subject to the potential 
$$
	V(\Phi) = -\mu^{2}\mathrm{Tr}[\Phi^{2}] + \lambda(\mathrm{Tr}[\Phi^{2}])^{2}.
$$
In this case, the symmetry breaking pattern is 
$$
	SO(3)\to SO(2),
$$
and the bosons acquire masses 
$$
	m_{W_{1}}^{2} = m_{W_{2}}^{2} \equiv m_{W}^{2},\quad m_{W_{3}}^{2}=0.
$$

This model could be added to the Standard model and change the value of the $\rho$ [[The rho Parameter|parameter]].
