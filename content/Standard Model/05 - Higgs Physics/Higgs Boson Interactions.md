In [[Electroweak Mass Generation]], we used the Higgs VEV $v$ to give masses to the $W, Z$, and fermions. However, we also need to consider the longitudinal mode $h$.

Because mass is generated by interacting with this exact same field, the Standard Model makes a testable prediction: **a particle's coupling to the Higgs boson must be strictly proportional to its mass.** 

## Self-Interactions
The Lagrangian of the Higgs doublet is given by 
$$
	\mathcal{L}_{H} = \lvert DH \rvert ^{2} - V(H^{\dagger}H),
$$
with
$$
V(H^{\dagger}H) = -\frac{1}{2} \mu^{2}(v+h)^{2} + \frac{\lambda}{4}(v+h)^{4}.
$$

Expanding this around the minimum ($v^{2} = \mu^{2}/\lambda$), we can extract the physical mass of the Higgs boson $h$:
$$
m_{h}^{2} = 2\lambda v^{2}.
$$

The remaining cubic and quartic terms describe how the Higgs boson interacts with itself. By substituting $\lambda = m_h^2 / 2v^2$, the self-interaction Lagrangian reads:
$$
\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{self-int}} = - \frac{m_{h}^{2}}{2v} h^{3} - \frac{m_{h}^{2}}{8v^{2}}h^{4}
$$
*(Note: These self-couplings are entirely determined by the Higgs mass and the VEV)*

## Fermion Couplings
In the unitary gauge, the Yukawa interactions couple the left- and right-handed fermions to the scalar field $(v+h)$. 

Using the mass definition $m_{\psi} = \frac{Y_{\psi} v}{\sqrt{2}}$ derived in [[Electroweak Mass Generation]], we get:
$$
\frac{Y_{\psi}}{\sqrt{2}} \bar{\psi}_{L}\psi_{R} (v+h) = m_{\psi} \bar{\psi}_{L} \psi_{R}\left( 1 + \frac{h}{v} \right)
$$
From this, we deduce that **the coupling between a fermion and the Higgs boson is exactly $m_\psi / v$.** The heavier the fermion (like the top quark), the stronger it interacts with the Higgs.

## Gauge Boson Couplings
For the $W$ and $Z$ bosons, the interactions arise from expanding the kinetic term $\lvert D_\mu H \rvert^2$ in the unitary gauge:
$$
\begin{align}
\lvert DH \rvert ^{2}  & = \frac{1}{2} (\partial h)^{2} + \left( \frac{g}{\sqrt{ 2 } }W_{\mu}^{+} \left(   \frac{v+h}{\sqrt{ 2 }}, \ 0\right) + \frac{1}{2} (g' B_{\mu} + g W_{\mu}^{3})\left( 0, \ \frac{v+h}{\sqrt{ 2 }} \right)\right)^{2} \\
 & = \frac{1}{2} (\partial h)^{2} + \frac{g^{2}}{4}W_{\mu}^{+}W_{\mu}^{-}(v+h)^{2} + \frac{g^{2}+g'^{2}}{8}Z_{\mu}Z_{\mu}(v+h)^{2}
\end{align}
$$
By expanding, we recover the mass terms (proportional to $v^2$), but we also get the interaction terms:
* **$VVh$ Couplings:** Proportional to $2v h$. Because $m_V^2 \propto v^2$, the coupling strength is $2m_V^2 / v$.
* **$VVhh$ Couplings:** Proportional to $h^2$.

> [!abstract] Observation: No Mixing in Unitary Gauge
> Notice that in this expansion, there are no kinetic mixing terms like $Z_{\mu} \partial^\mu h$. As discussed in [[Higgs Mechanism]], mixing terms only appear between the gauge bosons and the *Goldstone modes*.
