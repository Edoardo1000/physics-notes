In the Standard Model, couplings run:
$$
\begin{align}
 \delta m_{V} & \sim \ln\left( \frac{\mu}{\mu_{0}} \right)m_{W} \quad \mathrm{(the\ mass\ breaks\ gauge\ symmery)} ,\\
 \delta m_{f}  & \sim \log\left( \frac{\mu}{\mu_{0}} \right)m_{f}\quad \mathrm{(the\ mass\ breaks\ chiral\ symmetry)},
\end{align}
$$
while for scalars the variation is not necessarily proportional to the mass.

Now, suppose there is a theory which UV-completes the Standard Model beyond a certain scale $\Lambda$. It's going to generate corrections to the Higgs boson mass 
$$
	\delta m_{h}^{2} = \frac{1}{16\pi^{2}} \Lambda^{2}.
$$
Now, since $\Lambda\gg m_{h}$, we must necessarily have $\delta m_{h}^{2} \gg m_{h}^{2}$. Because of this, the experimental measured Higgs mass is 
$$
	(125 \ \mathrm{GeV})^{2} \sim m_{h}^{2} \biggr|_{\mathrm{\exp}} - \delta m_{h}^{2}.
$$
This is very strange: **I have cancellations of many digits**, this is unnatural.
